# Privacy policy: Only Natural

Last updated: November 2026

**Short version: Only Natural does not collect, store on a server, or send any personal data. Everything happens in your browser.**

## What the extension does with web pages

- It only acts on online shopping pages that list clothing. On every other page it does nothing.
- To find a product's material list, it loads that product's page from **the same shop you are already visiting** (the same request your browser would make if you opened the product). In some shops it opens the page in a hidden frame inside the current tab to let the shop's own scripts build it. These requests go only to that shop's website. They do not go to the developer or to any third party.
- It reads the material text (for example "80% cotton, 20% polyamide") and certification words from those pages, and uses them to show or hide products and to display labels.

## What is stored, and where

- **On your device only**, in the browser's extension storage:
  - your settings: on/off, how items that are not natural or cannot be read are handled (hide, or label only), and your per-website choices;
  - a cache of material results (product address and result) so that pages you have seen load faster; entries expire after 14 days;
  - short notes of which shops refused requests (the extension then pauses on that shop for 10 minutes) and which shops need their pages built in a hidden frame.
- **In private windows nothing is written to this storage.**
- Nothing is stored on any server of the developer. There is no account.

## What is not done

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies of its own.
- No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
- No remote code is downloaded or run. All code is included in the extension and is open source.

## Permissions, and why

- **Storage:** to keep the settings and the cache described above.
- **Access to all websites:** shops cannot be listed in advance, so the extension needs to run on whichever shop you open. It uses this only to read the product list and the material text on that shop's pages, as described above.

## Your control

- Switch the extension off at any time with the toolbar button, or switch it off for one website.
- Removing the extension deletes its stored settings and cache.

## Limits you should know about

Material readings come from the shop's own pages and can be wrong. They are not advice, and the extension is not a full sustainability rating.
